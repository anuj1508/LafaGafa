# Gum disease

Gum disease is common and, in its early stages, reversible.

## Signs

Bleeding when brushing, red or swollen gums, persistent bad breath, gums that have receded, or
teeth that feel loose. Bleeding gums are not normal, and are usually the earliest sign.

## Treatment

Early gum disease is treated by the hygienist: thorough cleaning above and below the gumline, and
a change in how you clean at home. Most cases improve substantially within a few months.

More advanced disease may need several appointments, more frequent hygienist visits — often every
three months rather than six — and occasionally referral to a periodontist.

## What we cannot promise

Gum disease is managed rather than cured. Bone lost around a tooth does not grow back. The aim is
to stop it progressing, which depends heavily on cleaning at home between appointments.

Smoking makes gum disease significantly worse and masks the bleeding that would otherwise warn
you.
