# About the practice

Northwind Dental has been on Fairfax Road since 2004. Four surgeries, six dentists, three
hygienists.

We see adults and children. Children under 16 are seen with a parent or guardian present for the
first visit.

## Getting here

Fairfax Road, two minutes from Northwind station. The 14 and 41 buses stop outside.

Four parking spaces behind the building, reached from Carter Lane rather than Fairfax Road. They
are often full mid-morning. There is metered street parking on Fairfax Road, free after 6pm.

## Access

Step-free from the street. Ground floor surgery available on request when booking — worth
mentioning at the time, as it cannot always be arranged on the day.

Hearing loop at reception.
