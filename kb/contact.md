# Contacting us

Reception is open during practice hours.

Email is answered within one working day. It is not monitored out of hours and should not be used
for anything urgent — call instead.

## What reception can help with

Booking, rescheduling, fees for listed treatments, membership questions, and directions.

## What they cannot

Reception cannot give clinical advice, say whether a symptom is serious, or estimate the cost of
treatment that has not been examined. Those questions are for a dentist, and are worth an
appointment rather than a phone call.
