# Teeth whitening

We offer take-home whitening. Custom trays are made from impressions taken at an appointment, and
the gel is applied at home over roughly two weeks.

£320, including the trays, the gel and a review appointment.

## Who it suits

An examination is required first. Whitening is not carried out on patients under 18, during
pregnancy, or where there is untreated decay or gum disease.

Whitening works on natural teeth. Crowns, veneers and white fillings do not change colour, so
existing work may need replacing afterwards to match — something worth discussing before starting
if you have visible work at the front.

## Results

Most people see a noticeable difference. How much varies and cannot be predicted beforehand.
Results last one to three years depending on diet and smoking; top-up gel can be bought from the
practice.

Sensitivity during treatment is common and temporary. Using the trays for shorter periods usually
settles it.
