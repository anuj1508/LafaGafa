# Opening hours

Monday to Thursday, 8:00am to 6:00pm.
Friday, 8:00am to 4:00pm.
Saturday, 9:00am to 1:00pm, for check-ups and hygienist appointments only.
Closed Sunday and on public holidays.

The last appointment of the day starts 30 minutes before closing.

## Out of hours

For urgent problems outside these hours, patients registered with us can call the practice number
and follow the emergency prompt. This reaches an on-call dentist, not the practice. There is a
call-out fee, which the on-call service confirms before attending.

For anything that is not urgent, book normally — the on-call service does not take bookings.
