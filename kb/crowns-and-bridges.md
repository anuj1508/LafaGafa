# Crowns and bridges

## Crowns

A crown covers a tooth that is too broken or too heavily filled to be restored otherwise, and
teeth that have had root canal treatment.

Two appointments about two weeks apart. The tooth is prepared and an impression taken at the
first; a temporary crown is worn in between. Temporary crowns come off occasionally — keep it and
call, it can usually be recemented.

£695. Materials vary and are discussed at the appointment.

## Bridges

A bridge replaces a missing tooth using the teeth either side as support. Suitable where those
teeth are sound and the gap is small.

From £1,200 depending on the number of units. Quoted after examination.

Bridges are not always the best answer for a missing tooth — implants and dentures are alternatives
with different costs and consequences. A dentist will go through them.

## Looking after them

Brush and floss normally. Bridges need cleaning underneath with interdental brushes or floss
threaders; the hygienist will show you how.
