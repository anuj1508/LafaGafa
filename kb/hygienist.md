# Hygienist appointments

Our hygienists see patients directly — a check-up with a dentist first is not required.

## What happens

Removal of plaque and tartar above and below the gumline, polishing, and advice on brushing and
cleaning between teeth. Stain removal where it is wanted.

Thirty minutes is right for most people. Forty-five is better for a first visit, for anyone who
has not seen a hygienist in over two years, or where there is heavy staining from tobacco,
coffee or red wine.

## How often

Most patients come every six months. Some are asked to come every three or four months, usually
where there is gum disease being managed. The hygienist advises at the appointment.

## Afterwards

Gums may be tender for a day or two and there can be some sensitivity to cold. Both settle. Avoid
anything that stains heavily for 24 hours after stain removal, which realistically means coffee,
tea, red wine and curry.
