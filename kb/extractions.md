# Extractions

Taking a tooth out is a last resort, considered when it cannot be saved or where keeping it would
cause more problems than removing it.

## The appointment

A simple extraction takes 20 to 30 minutes under local anaesthetic. Surgical extractions —
usually buried or broken teeth, and most wisdom teeth — are referred to an oral surgeon.

£180 for a simple extraction. Surgical extractions are quoted by the specialist practice.

## Afterwards

Bite on the gauze for 30 minutes. No rinsing, no hot drinks, no smoking and no alcohol for 24
hours; all of them disturb the clot that is forming.

Some bleeding and swelling over the first day or two is expected. Painkillers you would normally
take for a headache are usually enough.

## When to call us

Bleeding that has not settled after a few hours of firm pressure, or pain that worsens after
three days rather than improving. The second can be a dry socket, which is treatable at the
practice and settles quickly once dressed.
