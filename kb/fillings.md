# Fillings

We place white composite fillings as standard. Amalgam is available on request but is rarely used
now, and is not placed in children, during pregnancy, or in breastfeeding patients.

## The appointment

Thirty to forty-five minutes depending on the tooth. Local anaesthetic in most cases, though very
small fillings sometimes do not need it.

Afterwards the numbness lasts two to four hours. Avoid chewing on that side until it has gone —
it is easy to bite the inside of your cheek without noticing.

## Cost

£145 to £260. The range reflects the size of the filling and which tooth it is; back teeth take
longer and cost more. The exact figure is given after examination, before anything starts.

## How long they last

Most last five to ten years. They can wear, chip or leak at the edges, which is part of what a
check-up is looking for. A filling that has failed is usually replaced rather than repaired.

## Sensitivity afterwards

Mild sensitivity to cold for a week or two is normal, particularly on deeper fillings. Sensitivity
that is getting worse after two weeks, or pain on biting, is worth a call.
