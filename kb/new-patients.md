# New patients

We are taking new private patients.

## The first visit

A new patient examination, 40 minutes. It covers a full check of teeth, gums and soft tissues,
X-rays if they are needed, and a written treatment plan with costs if anything needs doing.

Bring a list of any medication. If you have had recent X-rays elsewhere, we can usually request
them rather than repeat them.

## Registering

Registration happens at the first appointment. There is no separate registration fee and no
waiting list.

## Nervous patients

Tell us when booking. We book longer appointments for anxious patients at no extra cost, and
several of our dentists have particular experience with dental phobia. Some treatments can be
done under sedation, which is assessed case by case at an appointment.
