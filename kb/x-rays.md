# X-rays

X-rays show what an examination cannot: decay between teeth and under fillings, bone levels, and
the roots of teeth.

## How often

Most patients have small X-rays every 18 to 24 months. More often where there is active decay or
gum disease being monitored, less often where everything is stable. Frequency is a clinical
decision made at the appointment, not a fixed schedule.

## Cost

£15 for a small X-ray, £85 for a panoramic. Both are included in a new patient examination when
they are needed, and in membership plan check-ups.

## Safety

Dental X-rays are a very low dose. A small X-ray is roughly comparable to a day or two of ordinary
background radiation.

We do not take X-rays during pregnancy unless there is an urgent clinical need, and we use a lead
apron when we do.

## Records from elsewhere

If you have had X-rays at another practice recently, tell us — we can usually request them rather
than repeat them.
