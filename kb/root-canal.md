# Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment saves a tooth whose nerve has died or become infected, rather than taking
the tooth out.

## What it involves

One or two appointments, each 60 to 90 minutes. The infected tissue is removed, the canals are
cleaned and shaped, and the space is filled and sealed. A tooth that has had root canal treatment
usually needs a crown afterwards to stop it fracturing.

## Cost

£450 for a front tooth, £750 for a molar. A crown afterwards is £695. Molars cost more because
they have more canals and take substantially longer.

## Does it hurt

The tooth is numbed throughout. Most patients find it comparable to having a filling — the
reputation comes from the pain of the infection beforehand rather than the treatment itself.

Some tenderness for a few days afterwards is normal, particularly on biting.

## Success

Most root canal treatments last many years. A small proportion fail and need retreatment or
extraction; this cannot be predicted at the outset.
