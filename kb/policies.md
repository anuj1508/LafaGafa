# Practice policies

## Medical history

We ask for a full medical history at the first visit and check it at every appointment after.
Please tell us about any change in medication, particularly blood thinners and bisphosphonates,
and about pregnancy.

## Referrals

Some treatment — surgical extractions, implants, orthodontics for adults — is referred to
specialists we work with. We make the referral; the specialist practice sets its own fees and
waiting times, which we do not control.

## Records

Requests for your dental records should be made in writing to the practice. Records are provided
within one month.

## Complaints

Complaints go to the practice manager, in writing or in person. They are acknowledged within
three working days. The full complaints procedure is available at reception on request.

## Chaperones

Available on request at any appointment, no reason needed.
