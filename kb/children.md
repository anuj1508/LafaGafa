# Children

We see children from their first tooth. Early visits are mostly about getting used to the place —
sitting in the chair, counting teeth, nothing more.

## Fees

Under 18s in full-time education are seen at a reduced rate: £35 for a check-up, £45 for a
hygienist appointment. Fillings and other treatment are quoted after examination.

## What we advise

Brushing twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste, supervised until around seven. Fluoride varnish
is applied at check-ups from age three, included in the fee.

Sealants on adult back teeth, usually around six or seven, are £45 per tooth.

## Nervous children

Tell us beforehand and we will book a longer appointment. Two of our dentists see children
routinely and are used to first visits that do not go to plan.
