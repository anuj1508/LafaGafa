# Payment plans

## Spreading the cost

Treatment over £500 can be split across three monthly payments, interest free, arranged at
reception before treatment starts. The first payment is taken on the day.

## Membership

Our practice plan is £24.50 a month and covers two check-ups, two hygienist appointments, and any
X-rays taken as part of them. It also gives 15% off other treatment.

Worth it for most patients who attend regularly; not worth it for anyone who comes once a year.
Reception can work it out against what you actually use.

Membership starts the month after joining and there is no minimum term.

## What is not included

The plan does not cover emergency assessments outside your regular appointments, treatment
started before joining, or whitening.
