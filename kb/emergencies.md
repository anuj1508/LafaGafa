# Dental emergencies

We keep emergency slots each weekday and aim to see registered patients in pain the same day.
Call as early as possible — the slots go quickly.

An emergency assessment is £95, which covers the examination and immediate pain relief. Any
treatment that follows is quoted separately.

## A knocked-out adult tooth

Time matters more than anything else here. Hold the tooth by the crown, never the root. If it is
dirty, rinse briefly in milk or saliva — not water, and do not scrub it. Put it back in the socket
if you can and bite gently on a clean cloth. If you cannot, keep it in milk and come straight in.

Baby teeth are not put back.

## Swelling

Facial swelling, particularly with fever or difficulty swallowing, needs to be seen urgently. If
swelling is affecting breathing or closing the eye, go to A&E rather than waiting for us.
