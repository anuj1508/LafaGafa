# Appointments

## Booking

Online, by phone, or in person. New patients are asked to arrive ten minutes early to complete a
medical history form; it can also be filled in beforehand if we have an email address on file.

## How long they take

- Check-up: 20 minutes
- Hygienist: 30 minutes
- Extended hygienist (first visit or heavy staining): 45 minutes
- Filling: 30 to 45 minutes depending on the tooth
- Emergency assessment: 20 minutes

## Cancelling

We ask for 24 hours' notice. Cancelling with less, or not turning up, is charged at £35 for a
check-up and £45 for a hygienist appointment. The fee is waived once per patient at the practice's
discretion, and is not charged for genuine emergencies.

## Running late

Arriving more than ten minutes late usually means the appointment has to be rebooked, because the
slot cannot be extended into the next patient's time. It is worth calling ahead — occasionally
there is room to see you anyway.
